# References and attribution

Thesio was informed by these public research products:

- [Narrative Alpha](https://github.com/bitnormie01/Narrative-Alpha): the idea of relating a financial narrative to candidate baskets.
- [Undertow](https://github.com/undertow-research/undertow): counterevidence and explicit invalidation conditions.
- [Minara](https://minara.ai/vi/app/marketplace): public examples of financial research questions.

These are conceptual references, not claims that their results were reproduced by Thesio. The reviewed Narrative Alpha and Undertow snapshots did not include a root license; their source archives are not distributed here. Public marketplace claims are not independently verified performance evidence.

Thesio implements source registration, hash checks, quote-span validation, financial-unit checks, a research ledger and a browser workspace. Any incorporated third-party material must retain its original attribution and permissions.

External SEC response bodies, uploaded filings and private research records are excluded from the public repository. Source metadata is not permission to redistribute a document. A hash verifies byte consistency, not authenticity or rights.
